The Trainer

Trained by experience. Driven by results.

Benita has spent the last seven years training dogs across South Africa — from anxious rescues to working line Malinois. Her approach blends classical obedience with the temperament work most trainers skip. The result is a dog that listens because it understands, not because it's been pressured into compliance.

Every dog walks into a session as an individual. Every plan she writes reflects that. If you've tried other methods and felt the dog wasn't quite there, this is the work that closes the gap.

  • 7+ years of professional canine training experience
  • Specialist in reactivity, aggression and working dogs
  • Hands-on with protection, sport and obedience disciplines
  • Based in Limpopo · Online programmes worldwide
